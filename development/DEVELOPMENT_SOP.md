# Development block SOP

Approved by Greg, October 29, 2026, Arizona.

1. Read the current source, unresolved owner failures, and deployment state. Pick concrete acceptance criteria and work through implementation, regression checks, browser QA and a reviewable checkpoint in one substantial session.
2. Prioritize recorded-data integrity and failing core flows. Keep The Pure and Life Pilot separate. Preserve original applications, real data, unknown fields and backups. Never seed recalled or fictional records as real history.
3. Use isolated fictional practice or sandbox data for routine QA. Tests must not secretly bypass the interaction being verified. Reproduce reported regressions before claiming repair where practical.
4. Update existing private candidates after passing checks under standing authorization. Do not merge, change public production, expand sharing, use paid services or spend money without specific authorization.
5. Keep dated source-controlled evidence: changes, commands, passed/failed results, browser observations, outstanding physical limitations and the single next implementation step. Do not equate test counts with release readiness.
6. Ask the owner only for necessary access or physical acceptance. Provide a current direct link and label Android/work machine/Chromebook. Do not ask for a real workout to substitute for routine software QA.
7. End each block with a concise completed/tested/blocked/next report. No work continues after the turn ends; do not imply unattended development or that remaining account credits can be measured or exhausted.

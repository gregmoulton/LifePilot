# The Pure HIT 1.6: Android acceptance checklist (for Greg)

Use this on your Android phone in Chrome, on the **preview link Claude gives you** (never the old Netlify links).
Tick each box. If anything looks wrong, write down the step number and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
Use **Practice mode** for steps 1–9, so nothing goes into your real history.

**Before you start:** wear your MOOFIT strap. Do not clear Chrome's site data for any Pure link.

| # | Do this | You should see | OK? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the link. Tap **Workout**. | The Workout Ready screen with **Connect HRM** and **Start Workout**. | ☐ |
| 2 | Tap **Connect HRM**, choose MOOFIT, tap Pair. | Your heart rate appears and changes every second or two. | ☐ |
| 3 | Tick **Practice session**, then tap **Start Workout**. | A 5-second countdown with tones, then **Push-ups**. | ☐ |
| 4 | Say "twelve reps" (or tap + to 12, then **Save reps**). | 12 saved on Push-ups, then a countdown to Jump Squats. | ☐ |
| 5 | Say "correction ten reps". | Push-ups changes to 10. | ☐ |
| 6 | Finish Squats and Rows (any numbers). | **Round complete / Recovering, wait for 110 BPM.** | ☐ |
| 7 | Rest. | When your HR stays at or below 110 for about 5 seconds: **READY** plus a tone. Conditioning does **not** start by itself. | ☐ |
| 8 | Tap **Pause**, wait 30 s, tap **Resume**. | The timer did not count the paused time. | ☐ |
| 9 | Tap **Start conditioning**, do Round 2, then **Finish cooldown & save**. | "Practice complete, excluded from history." | ☐ |
| 10 | **No-monitor test:** tap **Disconnect**, untick Practice session, tap Start Workout, tap **Continue without HRM**, finish Round 1. | A **No monitor? Use 2:00 timed recovery** button. | ☐ |
| 11 | Tap it. Try **−15 s** and **+15 s**. | The countdown changes. At 0: **READY**. Conditioning waits for your tap. | ☐ |
| 12 | Mid-Round 2, switch to another app for 20 s, then come back. | "Paused when Chrome went out of view." Your reps are still there. Tap Resume. | ☐ |
| 13 | Pull down to reload the page. | "Saved session restored." Same round, same reps. | ☐ |
| 14 | Tap **End workout**, then **Finish cooldown & save**. | "Partial workout, saved on this device." It appears under **History**. | ☐ |
| 15 | **History** tab. | The workout from step 14 is listed and the practice one is not. | ☐ |
| 16 | **Backup**, then **Save backup**. | A file `the-pure-local-history.json` in Downloads. | ☐ |
| 17 | Start another workout, then tap **Discard session**. | "Test discarded." History is unchanged. | ☐ |

**Not part of this test:** moving your real workout history from the old phone V2 app. That needs your separate approval first.
When it's approved, the steps will be: in the old app tap **Export results**, then in the new app go to **Backup**, choose the file, check the counts, and tap **Confirm merge**. The old app's data is never changed.

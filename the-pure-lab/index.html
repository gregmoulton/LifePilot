Workout rules

Test protocol v1

Each exercise, each round: perform as many controlled, technically sound reps as possible. Record the actual number. Stop when form deteriorates; never force reps to hit a number.

110 BPM is a trial data checkpoint, not medical clearance or a universal readiness rule. The 70% / 80% prescriptions and 130% unlock are on hold while this max-round experiment gathers data. Progression remains user-confirmed.
